# How the Platform Works

> A no-brokerage property platform — visitors find genuine, verified properties directly from owners, and connect on WhatsApp to close the deal themselves. No brokers. No brokerage fee.

---

## Who uses it

| User | What they do | Account needed? |
|---|---|---|
| **Visitor (seeker)** | Browses, searches, enquires | ❌ No — connects via WhatsApp |
| **Owner / Agency** | Registers, posts listings, gets verified | ✅ Yes |
| **Admin** | Verifies listings, manages users & plans | ✅ Yes |

---

## 1. Browsing — how visitors find properties

Visitors don't need to sign up. Everything is browsed **based on location / place**, under four simple modes:

- 🏠 **Rent** — properties available to rent
- 💰 **Buy** — properties for sale
- 📄 **Lease** — properties available on lease
- 🏢 **Agencies** — browse by a specific owner or agency

Within these, properties fall into **5 types:**

`PG` · `Hostel` · `Apartment` · `Villa / House` · `Land`

**Extra ways to search:**
- **By place** — search a specific area or town
- **By agency** — see all listings from one owner/agency
- **On the map** — browse properties visually by location

---

## 2. The visitor journey (finding → enquiring → closing)

Simple, no sign-up, a few taps from browsing to talking to an owner:

1. Visitor browses by **Rent / Buy / Lease / Agency**, filtered by location.
2. Opens a property's **details page** (photos, price, location).
3. Taps the **Enquire** button.
4. This opens the **WhatsApp bot**, which replies automatically with the **property location** and the **owner's phone contact**.
5. Visitor **contacts the owner directly** and closes the deal — no middleman.

> **Free-contact limit:** each visitor gets **3 free owner contacts** to start. After that, further contacts become a paid step (switched on later, once the platform is established).

---

## 3. Owners & agencies (listing properties)

The supply side — the only users who register.

1. **Simple registration** with basic information creates an owner/agency profile.
2. From the website, they get a **listing option** to post a property.
3. The listing goes to the **admin for verification** — not public yet.
4. Once approved, it's **officially listed** on the website.
5. Owners can **edit, update, or delete** their listings any time (full CRUD control).

> ⭐ **Important rule:** owners and agencies can currently list **only in the PG and Hostel categories.**
> The other types (Apartment, Villa/House, Land) appear in browsing but are **not open for owner self-listing yet.**

---

## 4. Admin side

The control room that keeps the platform genuine and running.

| Admin area | What it does |
|---|---|
| **User management** | View and manage owners, agencies, admins |
| **Property management** | Review, approve, reject, edit, remove listings |
| **Verification** | Check ownership → approve the listing to go live |
| **Subscription management** | Manage plans and paid features (for later) |

---

## The whole flow at a glance

```
Owner/Agency registers
   → posts a PG or Hostel listing
      → admin verifies
         → listing goes live
            → visitor browses by Rent / Buy / Lease / Agency + location
               → opens details → taps Enquire
                  → WhatsApp bot shares location + owner contact
                     → visitor & owner talk directly
                        → deal closed  ✅
```
